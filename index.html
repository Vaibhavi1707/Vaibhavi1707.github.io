I am Vaibhavi Lokegaonkar, a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park. I have been fortunate to work on research under the guidance of Prof. Dinesh Manocha and Prof. Ramani Duraiswami at the GAMMA and PIRL labs.

My research focuses on text- and video-conditioned audio generation. I also have experience with LLM inference optimization through KV-cache management.

I'm open to collaborations, so feel free to drop an email.
