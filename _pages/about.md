Hi! I am Vaibhavi Lokegaonkar, a graduate student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park (UMD CS). I work at the Perception and Intelligence Research Lab (PIRL) and the GAMMA Lab, advised by Prof. Dinesh Manocha and Prof. Ramani Duraiswami.

My research lies at the intersection of audio, vision, and language, with a focus on Music Audio Understanding, Audio-Visual Large Language Models (AV-LLMs), and Benchmarks for audio-centric intelligence

I am especially interested in developing methods that improve audio understanding, multimodal alignment, and reasoning efficiency.
